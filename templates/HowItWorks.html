How RentWise Works

RentWise leverages historical rental data from 2020 to 2024 collected from the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC). It uses a two-step predictive modeling approach:

Trend Estimation

A linear regression model captures the overall rental price trends over time.

Residual Correction

An XGBoost model predicts the remaining variations (residuals) that the linear trend doesn’t explain.

When a user inputs the year, room type, and zone, the app constructs a feature vector, feeds it into these models, and combines the outputs to produce an estimated rent value.

This hybrid approach allows RentWise to account for both linear trends and complex, non-linear factors, resulting in more accurate and reliable rent predictions for various room types and neighborhoods.
